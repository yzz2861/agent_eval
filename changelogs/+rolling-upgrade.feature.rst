Adds rolling worker upgrades: `WorkerManager.upgrade()` replaces server workers one generation at a time (limited by `max_concurrent`), only retiring an old process after its replacement has acked and passed an optional readiness check. Failed replacements stop the rollout, keep the healthy old generation serving, and clean up leftover children. The operation has a stable id, is idempotent against duplicate requests, and its status (generation, completed items, failure reason) is queryable via `WorkerManager.upgrade_status()`, the worker state, and the inspector.
